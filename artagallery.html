ABOUT ARTGALLERY

Art connects people.

ArtGallery is a multi-user online art community where registered artists can upload and showcase their own artwork. Visitors can explore, like and discover creators.

This single-file version keeps the visual design and common interactions in one HTML file. Because it has no PHP server or MySQL connection, its demo data is stored in your browser using localStorage.
